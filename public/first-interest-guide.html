Understanding Your First Interest Payment

A complete guide to how your first EMI is calculated using the 30/360 day convention

💡 What is the First Interest Payment?

When you participate in a P2P loan deal on OxyLoans, your first interest payment covers only the partial period from your participation date to the deal's first scheduled payment date.

From the 2nd month onwards, you receive a full month's interest every month until the deal closes, when your principal is also returned.

Why is the first payment different from other months? Your first payment covers the exact days between your participation date and the first payout date — it is not always a full 30 days. If you join close to the payout date, the first payment will be smaller than a regular monthly payment. If the gap between your participation date and the first payout date is more than 30 days, the first payment will actually be larger than a regular monthly payment.

📐 The 30/360 Day Convention

Industry Standard OxyLoans uses the 30/360 day count convention — every month is treated as exactly 30 days, and a full year as 360 days. This is the RBI-accepted standard for P2P lending in India.

This means:

  • January (31 days) → treated as 30 days
  • February (28 days) → treated as 30 days
  • Any day past 30 (e.g. 31st) → counted as the 30th
  • All months are equal — no advantage to investing in a short month

🔢 How Your First Payment is Calculated — Step by Step

1

Cap each date's day at 30

Under the 30/360 convention, no day goes past 30. If you joined on the 31st, it's treated as the 30th.

partDay = min(your participation day, 30)
emiDay = min(first payment day, 30)
2

Count the gross days between the two dates

Apply the standard 30/360 formula across years, months, and days:

grossDays = (emiYear − partYear) × 360
            + (emiMonth − partMonth) × 30
            + (emiDay − partDay)
3

Subtract 2 days (standard P2P convention)

By industry convention, both the participation day itself and the last day are excluded from the count.

firstDays = grossDays − 2

This is consistent practice across all Indian P2P platforms.

4

Calculate the daily interest rate

monthlyInterest = investedAmount × monthlyRate%
dailyRate = monthlyInterest ÷ 30
5

Multiply days × daily rate

firstPayment = firstDays × dailyRate

Shown to the paise in the calculator so that every date change shows a different result.

✏️ Worked Example

Scenario: You invest ₹50,000 at 1.75% per month. You join on 11 Sep 2026. The first payment date is 30 Sep 2026.

Calculation

Step 1 — Cap days at 30 partDay = min(11, 30) = 11  |  emiDay = min(30, 30) = 30
Step 2 — Gross days (same month) 30 − 11 = 19 days
Step 3 — Subtract 2 grace days 19 − 2 = 17 days counted
Step 4 — Monthly interest ₹50,000 × 1.75% = ₹875.00/month
Step 4 — Daily rate ₹875.00 ÷ 30 = ₹29.17/day
Step 5 — First Payment 17 × ₹29.17 = ₹495.83
From month 2 onwards:

₹50,000 × 1.75% = ₹875.00 every month for the remaining duration. Principal returned in the last month.

🔍 About Rounding — Why the Same ₹ Amount for Different Dates?

For very small investment amounts (e.g. the minimum ₹500), the daily interest rate is very small:

₹500 × 1.75% = ₹8.75/month
₹8.75 ÷ 30 = ₹0.2917/day

17 days × ₹0.2917 = ₹4.9583 → shows as ₹4.96
16 days × ₹0.2917 = ₹4.6667 → shows as ₹4.67
15 days × ₹0.2917 = ₹4.3750 → shows as ₹4.38

The OxyLoans Return Calculator shows the first payment to the paise (₹4.96, not ₹5) so that different participation dates always produce visibly different results.

What does OxyLoans actually credit? The actual credit to your wallet may be rounded to the nearest rupee by the system. The paise-level display in the calculator is to help you compare dates — the final payout figure may differ slightly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why does OxyLoans use the 30/360 convention?

It's the RBI-accepted standard for fixed-income instruments and makes calculations consistent across all months — no lender is advantaged or disadvantaged by which month they join.

Why subtract 2 days?

Both the first day of participation and the last day (payment date) are excluded from the interest count. This is a standard P2P lending convention and is applied consistently to all lenders in the same deal.

When exactly do I receive payments?

Payments are credited to your OxyLoans wallet on the deal's fixed payment date each month. You will receive an SMS and email notification for each credit.

Is the principal safe?

OxyLoans is an RBI-registered NBFC-P2P platform. As required by RBI regulations, no fund pool is maintained — lender funds are directly deployed to borrowers. Principal is subject to borrower repayment risk, which is mitigated through our credit assessment process.
